building it yourself, starting with this prompt

Build me a small native macOS menu bar app from scratch. Work directly on my Mac, show me progress after each step and iterate with me until I'm happy.
1. Stack: Swift and SwiftUI as a Swift Package (macOS 13 or later), plus a build script that produces a runnable .app bundle.
2. The app lives in the menu bar and keeps the Mac awake when asked, using IOKit power assertions. Off lets the Mac sleep normally again.
3. Modes: On (stay awake until turned off), Sunset (a countdown timer with a slider), End at (a time of day in 15-minute steps; if that time has passed, it ends tomorrow), and Until (stay awake while a chosen running app is running).
4. The menu: On, Off, Sunset with its timer, slider and countdown, End at with minus/plus, Until as a single row with a window icon and a chevron that opens a submenu of running apps (do not list the apps inline in the main menu), Start at login, Countdown in menu, Settings with a gear icon, Quit.
5. Settings is a separate window (not named Appearance): themes and highlight colors with a live preview, a keyboard shortcut that switches between off and the last On, Sunset or Until mode (Escape cancels, Clear removes it), Allow display sleep (the display may sleep while the Mac stays awake), Turn off on battery (plugging in does not turn Awake back on), and Check for Updates.
6. Keep power-assertion and mode logic separate from the UI and cover it with unit tests (swift test).
7. When it works, build the .app with the script, launch it, and show me screenshots of the menu (including the Until row) and Settings.

Requires macOS 13 or later. One-time purchase, no subscription.

On

Leave it on. The Mac stays awake until you turn Awake off.

Off

Turn it off and the Mac can sleep again, the way it did before.

Sunset

A timer, instead of forever. When it ends, Awake lets the Mac sleep.

End at

A time of day, in 15-minute steps. If that time has passed, it ends tomorrow.

Until

Awake while a chosen app is running. When that app quits, Awake turns off.

Start at login

Awake is already in the menu bar when you sign in.

Countdown in the menu bar

Show the time left beside the icon. Hide it when you want the bar quiet.

Shortcut

Switches between off and your last On, Sunset, or Until. Escape cancels. Clear removes it.

Allow display sleep

The display can sleep. The Mac stays awake.

Turn off on battery

On battery, Awake turns off. Plugging in does not turn it back on.

Settings

Themes and accent colors for the menu. The one above uses a white accent. Check for Updates is in this window.
